Theorem Tiles Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

Short version: Theorem Tiles keeps puzzle progress on your device. There is no Theorem Tiles account. Purchases go through Apple. Face ID is optional and only for the Grown-ups vault after you set a PIN.

Who we are

Theorem Tiles is published by TJNAI.ai LLC (“we”, “us”). Contact: info@tjnai.ai.

What the app stores on your device

  • Play progress — completed puzzles, grove unlocks, and related local preferences.
  • Grown-ups PIN — stored in the iOS Keychain on your device when you enable it.
  • Purchase state — whether optional content is unlocked, as reported by Apple’s StoreKit on-device.

Face ID / Touch ID

If you enable biometric unlock for the Grown-ups area, iOS evaluates Face ID or Touch ID locally. We never receive your biometric data — only a yes/no unlock result from the system.

Purchases

Optional unlocks use Apple In-App Purchase. Payment details are handled by Apple, not by TJNAI.ai LLC. Restore Purchases uses Apple’s standard StoreKit restore flow.

What we don’t do

  • No Theorem Tiles user accounts or passwords.
  • No third-party advertising SDKs in the kid play path.
  • No sale of personal information.
  • We do not build advertising profiles from child play data.

Children

Theorem Tiles is designed for families. Grown-ups controls sit behind a PIN (and optional biometrics). We do not require a child to provide contact information to play.

Analytics & crash data

If we enable Apple’s standard privacy-preserving diagnostics or similar first-party tooling in a future build, it will be disclosed here before use. Today’s shipping intent is on-device progress without third-party trackers.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove local progress from that device.
  • Manage Face ID / Screen Time in iOS Settings.
  • Manage subscriptions and purchase history in your Apple ID settings.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. We’ll revise the effective date above when we do.

Contact
